Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This Privacy Policy explains how Whiteboard: Draw & Notes handles your drawings, documents, information, and permissions.

Effective Date: October 15, 2026

Privacy at a Glance

Whiteboard: Draw & Notes is designed as an offline-first whiteboard application. You can create drawings, handwritten notes, diagrams, and local projects without signing in or connecting to the internet.

Local whiteboard content remains on your device unless you choose to export, share, back up, or use an optional online feature.

App Package: com.digital.whiteboard

1. Introduction

Welcome to Whiteboard: Draw & Notes ("the App"), developed by [DEVELOPER NAME].

This Privacy Policy describes how information is accessed, processed, stored, and shared when you use the App.

By using the App, you acknowledge the data practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information Processed by the App

The App processes information necessary to provide its whiteboard, drawing, annotation, and document features.

Data Type Purpose Storage
Drawings and handwriting Creating and editing whiteboards Local device
Notes and text Creating editable notes Local device
Images and PDF documents Importing and annotating files Local device
Project information Saving and organizing boards Local device
App preferences Remembering user settings Local device
Audio recordings, when enabled Recording whiteboard lessons or narration Local device unless explicitly shared

The core offline editor does not require you to provide your name, email address, or other account information.

3. Local Data Storage

Whiteboard projects, handwritten notes, drawings, shapes, and imported documents are designed to be stored locally on your Android device.

Local storage allows you to use the application without an internet connection.

You can manage your saved projects using the available application controls.

Exported files or Android system backups may be stored separately according to your device and backup settings.

4. Device Permissions

Depending on the features available in your installed app version, the following device capabilities may be requested:

Files and Documents

Used to import, open, save, and export whiteboards, PDFs, images, and other supported documents.

Camera

If the camera feature is available, camera access may be requested when you choose to capture an image for your whiteboard.

Microphone

If audio recording is supported, microphone access may be requested when you start a recording.

Internet

Internet connectivity may be used for optional online collaboration, cloud synchronization, or AI features if these services are enabled.

You can manage applicable permissions through your Android device settings.

5. Sharing and Exporting Data

You may choose to export or share your whiteboards, images, PDF documents, or recordings with other applications.

When you share content using Android's sharing functionality, the selected destination application or service may receive that content.

Information shared with another service is subject to that service's own privacy practices.

We do not sell your whiteboard content or personal information.

6. Optional Cloud Collaboration

Note: This section applies only if optional cloud collaboration or account features are enabled in your installed version.

The App may offer optional cloud synchronization and collaboration features.

If you choose to use these features, the App may process:

  • Account identifiers
  • Email addresses used for authentication
  • Selected whiteboard content
  • Collaboration changes
  • Sharing permissions
  • Information needed to synchronize projects

Cloud features are optional and are not required for local whiteboard editing.

When these features become available, this policy will be updated with the relevant service providers, data retention practices, and deletion procedures.

7. Optional AI Features

Certain versions of the App may offer optional artificial intelligence tools, including handwriting recognition, text recognition, diagram generation, and document analysis.

When AI processing occurs directly on your device, the selected content can be processed locally.

If an optional cloud-based AI provider is configured, selected text, images, handwriting, or other submitted content may be transmitted to that provider to complete your request.

The App should inform you before transmitting private content to an external AI service.

You can continue using the core offline whiteboard without using cloud AI features.

8. Third-Party Services

The App may use software libraries and Android system services required to provide its functionality.

If third-party services such as analytics, crash reporting, authentication, cloud storage, or online AI processing are enabled, this policy will be updated to describe the relevant providers and their data practices.

Any third-party service integrated into the released application must be accurately disclosed where required.

9. Advertising and Payments

The intended free version of Whiteboard: Draw & Notes does not include in-app advertisements, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.

We do not require payment information to access the core whiteboard features.

If this changes in a future release, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain the relevant practices.

10. Data Retention

Locally stored whiteboard projects, notes, imported documents, and settings generally remain on your device until you delete them or remove the application's stored data.

Uninstalling the application normally removes app-private local data, subject to Android backup settings and copies stored elsewhere.

Files that you exported to other locations must be managed separately.

If cloud services become available, applicable server-side retention periods will be disclosed.

11. Data Deletion

You can remove locally saved whiteboards and projects using the deletion controls provided in the application.

You can also clear the application's local data through your Android device settings.

If optional user accounts are introduced, appropriate account deletion functionality and a web-based deletion request method will be provided as required by Google Play.

To ask questions about deletion, contact us using the email listed below.

12. Data Security

We aim to use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by the application.

Local project data is designed to use Android's application storage protections.

Optional online services should use encrypted connections and appropriate access controls.

However, no storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

We encourage users to protect their devices and review the security settings of services used for sharing.

13. Children's Privacy

Whiteboard: Draw & Notes is a general-purpose drawing, note-taking, and educational productivity application.

The core local whiteboard functionality does not require users to create accounts or submit personal information.

If online features designed for children or features processing children's personal information are introduced, we will follow applicable privacy requirements and Google Play policies.
